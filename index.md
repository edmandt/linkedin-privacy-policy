# Privacy Policy – Personal LinkedIn Publisher

**Effective Date:** 13 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Personal LinkedIn Publisher is an application designed to allow its authorised user to create, review and publish content to their personal LinkedIn profile through LinkedIn's API.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, processes, stores and protects personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you authorise the application through LinkedIn OAuth 2.0, the application may access:

- Your LinkedIn member identifier.
- Your basic profile information, where authorised.
- Your email address, if the relevant permission is granted.
- LinkedIn OAuth access tokens.
- Content you create and approve for publication.

The application does not request or store your LinkedIn password.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

- Authenticate your LinkedIn account.
- Identify the account authorised to publish content.
- Create and publish LinkedIn posts following your approval.
- Maintain the security and functionality of the application.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored securely using appropriate access controls and encryption where applicable.

Only the application operator has access to the information required to operate the application.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Information is shared with LinkedIn only as necessary to authenticate the user and publish approved content.

We do not sell or disclose personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information and authentication tokens are retained only for as long as necessary to operate the application.

Users may request deletion of their information by contacting the application operator.

LinkedIn access can also be revoked through LinkedIn account settings.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The application uses LinkedIn's API and authentication services.

LinkedIn's handling of personal information is governed by its own privacy policy:

https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, users may have rights to access, correct, restrict or request deletion of their personal information.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application or applicable legal requirements.
